Learner Lab Arcade

Four team quiz games on language aptitude and motivation, written for LING/GER/RSSS 114, How to Learn a Foreign Language. Each game has 21 questions in five levels that get harder and are worth more points, including a memory sprint with Sorani Kurdish words. At the end, every question is shown with its correct answer and an explanation.

  • 1. Learner ProfilesThe classic set, with nature words in the memory sprint.
  • 2. Aptitude ArenaNew scenarios, with body and nature words.
  • 3. Motivation MatchNew learners and stories, with food words.
  • 4. Language Lab ChallengeThe hardest mix, with animal words.
  1. Each group opens the games on one phone or laptop.
  2. Type your group's name and choose a game.
  3. The timer starts as soon as a question appears. Click an answer; faster correct answers earn a bonus.
  4. Show your final score screen to your instructor.
