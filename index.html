I am a Ph.D. candidate in Linguistics at the University of Arizona (expected 2027), and I also hold an M.S. in Human Language Technology. My research connects theoretical syntax with computational modeling.

On the theoretical side, I study case, agreement and alignment in Iranian languages, especially Kurdish varieties, Balochi and Persian. On the computational side, I study how large language models represent grammatical structure, work on tokenization for LLMs, and build NLP tools for low-resource, morphologically complex languages.

My dissertation, Alignment in Human Language and Large Language Models: Evidence from Iranian Languages, brings these two lines of work together.

I am on the job market for 2026–27, for both academic and industry positions.
